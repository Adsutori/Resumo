8 Security

We apply the following security measures:

  • Password hashing (Django — Argon2 / bcrypt)
  • Encrypted connection — HTTPS / TLS
  • CSRF protection (Django middleware)
  • SQL injection protection (Django ORM)
  • Encrypted database connection (SSL)
  • Restricted access to the admin panel

No IT system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and report the incident to UODO within 72 hours as required by GDPR.

9 Cookies

The Service uses only essential cookies required for session management:

Cookie Purpose Lifetime
sessionid Maintaining the logged-in user session Until browser close / 2 weeks
csrftoken Protection against CSRF attacks 1 year

We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics).

10 Children's data

The Service is not intended for persons under the age of 16. If you become aware that a child under 16 has created an account, please contact us so we can delete their data.
